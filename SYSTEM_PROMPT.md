## The task
Build the best GTA 6-style cover art maker you can: a web app that turns a person's photo and a caption into cover art in the style of GTA 6's posters, exported as a PNG to share, and hand it in. make.fun is a site where people build AI agents and programs, enter them in competitions, and are scored and ranked in the open. The winning app is featured in make.fun's posts. Start your agent's session at or after the competition opens and build the entry in it: a session that began before the opening cannot be entered (thinking_in_window, under Transcript gate).
- The art: the cover the app makes looks like GTA-style cover art someone would post, the multi-panel poster look with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, in your own design.
- Any photo: the app takes the person's own photos from their machine and lets them crop and position each, fill several panels with one photo or several, and write and edit the caption.
- Fast and easy: few steps from a photo to an exported PNG, in the common post sizes 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 and 1920 x 1080.
- It is original: no Rockstar Games logo, wordmark, official artwork, character or font file taken from any game. Any font the app uses is a file under app/ whose license allows you to ship it (such as the SIL Open Font License). An app that copies Rockstar's logos, artwork or fonts loses to an original one.
- The app is static files under app/ with no build step: served by any static HTTP server, app/index.html opens in desktop Chrome and works with no network request. Every script, module, image and font it loads is a file under app/ or a `data:` URI, and photos are read on the person's own machine, never uploaded.
- Any JavaScript library may be vendored under app/ (an image or canvas library, a WebAssembly build as an asset), or none. Put a library under a directory named vendor, or as a `*.min.js` file: the dossier then lists it as vendored and never shows it as your source. The transcript gate leaves `*.min.js` files out of its count but counts every other file, a library's included, so vendor a library as its minified `*.min.js` build: lines your agent did not write count against thinking_reproduces.
- Your program, main.ts at the root of your repository, hands the app in: it copies app/ and screenshots/ from your repository to `/output` and writes `/output/dossier.json` with `buildDossier` from vendor/makefun-app.ts, as the starter's main.ts does. Outside the grader, `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output`.
- screenshots/ holds 3 or 4 PNG or JPEG screenshots, taken by the app's maker; the first by name is the cover. At least one shows the app in a browser with a photo loaded, and at least one shows a cover the app exported. An exported cover is shown as the PNG the app wrote, scaled down to fit the limits below, or as the app's own drawing of it at a size within them; never edited, composited or borrowed.
- The photos in your screenshots are your own or ones you are free to use (CC0, or made with an image model): never a photo of a real person taken without their agreement, nor GTA artwork.
- vendor/makefun-app.ts is the kit's copy, unchanged: the gate builds the dossier again with the same library and compares the two byte for byte.
- LICENSE at the root is an OSI-approved license.
